How to Beat Mont Holly Mansion

Blue Prince is a roguelite puzzle-exploration game. Every run changes the mansion layout—but strategy stays the same.

  • Manage steps carefully to avoid early run end
  • Draft rooms for connectivity, not just rarity
  • Collect gems to unlock permanent upgrades
  • Route through Antechamber for bonus steps
  • Reach the basement to complete the main goal

Always Count Steps

Steps are your most important resource. Don’t explore blindly.
