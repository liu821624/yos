Items & Collectibles

All resources, shop items, and stamps you'll find in Mont Holly

Shop Items

  • Boots: Reduces step cost for moving between rooms
  • Compass: Reveals the location of the Temple early
  • Key Ring: Doubles the number of keys you can carry

Stamps

Stamps are permanent modifiers that affect your entire run. Collecting multiple stamps of the same type unlocks powerful bonuses.

The 9 Gems

Collecting all 9 gems is required to unlock the true ending. Each gem is hidden in a different part of the mansion, requiring specific rooms or items to reach.
