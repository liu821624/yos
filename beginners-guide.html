Beginner's Guide

Everything you need to know to survive your first visit to Mont Holly Mansion

Core Game Loop

Every playthrough of Blue Prince is a unique mansion. You start at the entrance, and each day you add one new room to the mansion from four available options. Your goal is to reach the Temple at the top floor, collecting gems and uncovering secrets along the way.

Step Management

Steps are your most valuable resource. Every action costs steps, and once you run out, the day ends. Prioritize actions that give you permanent step bonuses or unlock new areas early.

Key Resources

  • Coins: Used to buy items in the Shop room
  • Keys: Open locked doors to new rooms
  • Stamps: Powerful modifiers that affect your entire run
  • Gems: Collect all 9 to unlock the true ending
