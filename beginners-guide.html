Beginner's Guide

Everything you need to survive your first run in Mont Holly Mansion.

Core Game Loop

Wake up in the Entrance

Every run starts fresh. You have limited steps.

Draft rooms at doorways

Choose 1 of 3 rooms to place. Doors must align.

Collect resources

Gather coins, gems, keys, and items.

Reach the Basement

Your main goal: find and enter B1.

Run ends, knowledge stays

Unlocked rooms carry over between runs.

Step Management

Steps = your life force. Running out ends the run instantly.

Plan every move

Don’t explore randomly. Always have a goal.

Antechamber is critical

Gives +10 steps. Prioritize it above almost everything.

Resource Priority

ResourcePriorityPurpose
GemsHighestUnlock permanent rooms
KeysHighOpen doors & basement
StepsCriticalKeep your run alive
CoinsMediumBuy shop items

First Run Goals

  • Find the Antechamber
  • Collect at least 2 gems
  • Get 1+ keys
  • Learn room door patterns
  • Don’t rush the basement
