Rooms Guide

Every room in Mont Holly Mansion, ranked by priority and use case

High Priority Rooms

Shop

Allows you to buy powerful items with coins. Place early to maximize your shopping days.

Library

Lets you reroll your daily room options, critical for finding key rooms when you need them.

Utility Rooms

Garden

Restores steps each day. Place near high-traffic areas for maximum efficiency.

Well

Grants a permanent step bonus when activated, great for long-term runs.
