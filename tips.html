Tips & Tricks

Pro strategies to help you beat Blue Prince consistently

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wasting steps: Don't explore every room on day 1. Focus on unlocking key areas first.
  • Ignoring the Shop: The Shop is the most powerful room in the game. Prioritize it above almost everything else.
  • Building dead ends: Plan your room placement so you never get stuck with no way to expand.

Winning Patterns

Most successful runs follow a similar pattern: unlock the Shop early, get step bonuses, then start collecting gems and working your way up to the Temple.

Daily Checklists

Before ending your day, make sure you've: used all coins, activated any one-time room bonuses, and checked if you can place a new room to unlock new paths.
